# ArrestIQ Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026

## Introduction

ArrestIQ (“the App”) is developed and published by Code 3 Coders (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the ArrestIQ mobile application.

ArrestIQ is a law enforcement reference and decision-support application designed to assist users with California criminal offense identification, bail schedule consultation, pre-arraignment release protocols, and custody disposition assessments.

The App provides structured workflows to assist law enforcement personnel in evaluating criminal charges, applicable bail amounts, release classifications, and relevant statutory considerations.

ArrestIQ is intended as an informational and operational support tool. It does not replace professional judgment, applicable laws, judicial orders, or agency policies.

## Information We Collect

ArrestIQ is designed with a local-first approach that prioritizes user privacy and data security.

The App does not require user account creation and does not independently collect or transmit personal information to servers operated by Code 3 Coders.

Information entered or generated within the App may include:

- Selected criminal offenses and statutory violations
- Criminal charge classifications and associated bail amounts
- Arrest and custody-related information
- Victim identifiers or descriptive labels
- Incident and offense grouping information
- Responses to custody and release eligibility questions
- Pre-arraignment release assessments
- Bail calculation results
- Saved or incomplete arrest assessment drafts

Information entered into the App is processed locally on the user's device to support its intended functionality.

ArrestIQ does not require users to enter personally identifiable information about arrestees or victims to perform its primary decision-support functions.

## Criminal Justice Information

ArrestIQ may be used in professional law enforcement environments where information relating to criminal investigations, arrests, or custody decisions is considered sensitive.

The App is designed to support the use of non-identifying information whenever practical.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary personally identifiable information, criminal justice information (CJI), or other sensitive data unless expressly authorized by their agency's policies and applicable requirements.

Code 3 Coders does not independently access, retrieve, or maintain criminal justice records entered into the App.

## Data Storage and Security

ArrestIQ uses a local-storage-focused architecture.

Arrest assessment information, selected charges, and saved drafts are stored locally on the user's device.

The App does not require cloud-based processing to perform its core bail calculation and custody decision-support functions.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security, including:

- Device passcodes
- Biometric authentication
- Device encryption
- Operating system security updates
- Device backup and synchronization settings
- Compliance with applicable agency mobile-device policies

Information stored locally may be subject to the user's device backup or synchronization configurations, including Apple services enabled by the device owner.

While reasonable efforts are made to design the App responsibly, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Sharing

ArrestIQ does not independently:

- Sell user information
- Share user information with advertisers
- Upload arrest assessment information to servers operated by Code 3 Coders
- Collect or transmit criminal history information for commercial purposes
- Use third-party analytics services to collect arrest assessment contents
- Submit arrest assessment information to external artificial intelligence processing services
- Provide remote access to locally stored arrest drafts

Any future functionality allowing users to export or share information will be subject to the user's direct action and applicable device permissions.

Users remain responsible for ensuring that any information they share is handled in accordance with applicable laws and agency policies.

## Bail Schedule and Legal Reference Data

ArrestIQ includes criminal offense information, bail schedule data, release protocol classifications, and related legal reference materials.

These resources are provided as part of the App's functionality and do not require the transmission of user-entered arrest information to Code 3 Coders.

The App may be updated periodically to reflect changes in applicable laws, court schedules, release protocols, and other reference materials.

Code 3 Coders does not guarantee that all legal reference information will remain current at all times.

Users are responsible for independently verifying applicable laws, judicial orders, bail schedules, and agency procedures before making official custody or release decisions.

## CJIS Considerations

ArrestIQ is designed with a local-storage-focused workflow intended to simplify agency review of Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) security considerations.

The App does not independently connect to criminal justice databases, retrieve criminal history records, or transmit user-entered arrest information to Code 3 Coders.

Local storage alone does not establish CJIS compliance.

Use of ArrestIQ in environments involving criminal justice information remains subject to applicable FBI CJIS Security Policy requirements, state regulations, and agency-specific information security procedures.

Individual users and agencies are responsible for determining whether their intended use of the App complies with applicable security, confidentiality, retention, and data-handling requirements.

Code 3 Coders does not represent ArrestIQ as independently certified or approved by the FBI, California Department of Justice, or any other criminal justice authority.

## Third-Party Services

ArrestIQ utilizes standard Apple iOS frameworks and system services necessary for application functionality.

These may include:

- Local application data storage
- User interface and navigation services
- Device security and operating system protections
- Apple App Store purchase and distribution services

Apple may independently process information associated with application downloads, purchases, device diagnostics, and other platform services according to its own privacy policies.

ArrestIQ does not require access to the device's camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location to perform its core functions.

## Data Retention and Deletion

ArrestIQ may retain incomplete arrest assessment drafts locally to allow users to resume their work.

Users may delete locally saved arrest drafts using the App's available reset functionality.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to any copies retained through device backups or other operating system services.

Code 3 Coders does not maintain remote copies of user-generated arrest assessments and therefore cannot independently retrieve or delete information stored solely on a user's device.

## Children's Privacy

ArrestIQ is intended for professional law enforcement personnel and adult users seeking access to criminal justice reference information.

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Code 3 Coders does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in application functionality, applicable laws, or data-handling practices.

Changes become effective when the updated Privacy Policy is published.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
